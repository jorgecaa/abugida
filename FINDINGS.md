# Findings

What the 2026-08-02 experiments established, and what they say about where to
spend effort next. Every row traces to a script under `eval/` or a test.
`TODO.md` holds the same material as working notes; this is the summary.

**Read every "recall" below as agreement with DPD** (decided 2026-10-13): the
share of the 5,002 pairs in DPD's `var_phonetic`, August 2026 build, that a
setting also generates. That field is computed by DPD's own phonetic rules,
not curated from the texts, so these rows say how far the metric agrees with
one rule system, and not whether the
metric is right. The decisions closed on them are not shown wrong, only
supported by that kind of number; the grammar-based checks (`eval/oberlies.py`,
`eval/geiger.py`), the open-vocabulary task and the hand-written battery are
the independent ones; `eval/hard_battery.py` is not, its triples being
harvested from `var_phonetic` pairs.
README.md and docs/comparator.md say the same under "Agreement with DPD is
not accuracy".

## What was discovered

| finding | evidence | why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **The pipeline beats the metric.** Phonological normalisation + a lexical criterion lift variant recall 63.6% → 82.0% at 0.92 cand/lemma, where the metric alone needs 6.6 cand/lemma to reach 83.9% | `eval/variant_curve.py`, Phase 1–2 | The single most useful fact for an integrator. Published in `docs/comparator.md` |
| **Two of its three components were already built and simply not wired up** — `assimilate_word` (+5.3 pts) and `contract_word` (+8.6) | Phase 2 | Check what exists before adding anything |
| **And now they are wired up**: `Lexicon(words, normalise=True)` gives 62.0% → 77.5% end-to-end, +6 to +13 points at MATCHED candidate volume | `abugida/lexicon.py` | The largest single improvement in the package, and it is an API decision rather than a metric change |
| **`construction` is the cheapest recall in the system**: +8.2 pts for +0.082 cand/lemma, an order of magnitude better than buying the same from θ | `Lexicon.variants(constructions=)` | It reaches pairs no threshold can: `abhikkhaṇaṃ ~ abhiṇhaṃ` (2.37) |
| **The tradition predicts.** ṭhāna/karaṇa doctrine said guttural~palatal should be confusable; the corpus then agreed (11.4% of place substitutions, 90.1% on the metric-independent count) | `eval/karana_bridges.py` | The only method that produced a real *metric* improvement all session |
| **The geminate transitions were load-bearing**: removing them cost 15.1 recall points and moved 20.5% of true pairs, always for the worse. The heavy units that replaced them (2026-10-13) are worth 14.4 points at ONE_MOVE on the 5,143-pair build (62.6% → 48.2% without), and every one of the 1,262 pairs that changes gets worse | `eval/ablate_geminates.py` | They do not add recall — they make it affordable, at 2–5× less volume |
| **A lattice cannot express cyclicity.** `or_`/`and_`/`not_` form a De Morgan algebra but not a boolean one, so the derived sum is not xor | `TestLatticeOps` | The modifier being a *field* is structural, not stylistic |
| **The ring §1.5 rules out is exactly Z/6Z**, reachable by making the base xor — at the price of one axiom | `TestXorVariant` | Prices the axiom: subtraction would mean voicing is reversible |
| **The benchmark was scoring against its own hypothesis.** `CONFUSION_PAIRS` listed tongue-tip confusions but not tongue-body ones, so the dorsal bridge could only lose there | Phase 3 | Corrected, the apparent task conflict stops being significant (p 0.0039 → 0.2266) |
| **The hand-written battery is saturated**: 29/30 (30/30 since the heavy units), and it holds that even when `a↔ā` is driven to 0.073 | Phase 0 | It detects regressions only. Use `eval/hard_battery.py` (50.6%, ample headroom) |
| **The deployment threshold had drifted in two directions at once.** README said p*=50%, SPEC and comparator.md said 63%; re-derived it is **56.8% [45.4, 68.7]** | `eval/decision_curve.py` | Deploy the hybrid iff your typo share is below it. The number moves whenever the metric does — quote the script, not a remembered figure |
| **On equal terms Levenshtein is much closer than published.** With normalisation and a relative threshold available to both, the metric's margin is +5.6 points at 1 cand/lemma and +7.4 at 2, even at 4 (+1.0), −1.0 at 8 (re-run 2026-10-13 after the heavy units; it was +3 at 4, +7 at 2) — and dressed Levenshtein beats the BARE metric by 9 | `eval/head_to_head.py` | The metric buys RESOLUTION (it runs below 4 cand/lemma, where integer costs cannot), the pipeline buys recall. The old comparison withheld from the baseline what it granted itself |
| **A length-relative threshold is worth +2 to +9 points at matched volume.** Recall falls 84.2% → 72.2% from short words to long ones under a fixed θ; scaling the budget with the word fixes most of it | `Lexicon.variants(relative=True)` | Predicted by the noisy-channel reading before measuring. The ratio is NOT a metric (6 triangle violations in 314k triples), so it cuts candidates and never indexes them |
| **A threshold now has a probabilistic reading.** Inverting the fitted channel gives θ for a target precision; on DPD `ONE_MOVE` admits ~12%, and one algebra move lands within half a percent of the 25% threshold | `abugida/channel.py` | Turns θ from a structural number into a decision the integrator can make against a precision budget |
| **The linear cost is derived, not defaulted.** Under the noisy-channel reading (cost = −log P), confusability decays exponentially over the operating range: R² 0.974 identity vs 0.932 log at d ≤ 1.5, identity winning at every cut through 2.5 | `eval/noisy_channel.py` | Closes the last "we simply never changed it" in the metric. The concave warp is not just unprofitable — it is wrong for the observed decay |
| **The "below chance at three edits" deficit was a measurement artefact.** Raw, lev=3 scores 19.9%; through the recommended pipeline the same triples score **79.5%** | `eval/hard_battery.py` | Those triples are `aya↔e` alternations, exactly what `contract_word` normalises. Scoring the metric in isolation misdiagnosed the project's own recommendation as a hole in the metric |
| **Against Levenshtein the margin is unchanged and now better measured**: scribal corruption 95.5% [93.0, 97.1] vs 83.9% at n=400; sandhi 82.5% vs 77.1%; tadbhava 58.3% vs 36.1% | `eval/compare_levenshtein.py` | Only row 1 has the sample to resolve anything — n=40 spans ±11.6 points, n=12 ±24.4 |

| **The word-level triangle inequality now holds — one segmentation per word** (2026-10-13). It failed across the geminate alignment transitions (`d("makka","saṅgha")` 2.262 > 2.065 through `magga`, 86 violations in 26,400 triples), all from one cause: each comparison chose whether a geminate was one long consonant or two letters. Raising the unit costs or closing the transitions under composition does not fix it; heavy units do (geminates and homorganic nasal+stop clusters as single tokens, priced by the max over their halves and weight) | `eval/triangle_geminates.py`, `tests/c/test_edge_cases.c` | A true metric by theorem: battery 30/30, Pāli doublets unchanged; the price is Sanskrit-to-Pāli correspondences (within two moves 40.0% → 31.6% Oberlies, 47.8% → 35.0% Geiger; the Geiger tadbhava ranking 32.8% → 24.5%, paired, McNemar p < 0.01); agreement with DPD at ONE_MOVE only 63.2% → 62.6% on one build |
| **The structural ceiling is 3.2%, not the 10% a raw distance cut suggests.** 503 `var_phonetic` pairs (10.0%) sit at d > 1.0, but the length-relative threshold reaches 71% of them; 159 (3.2%) are genuinely unreachable | direct query against DPD | Two lessons. The relative threshold is worth more than its +2–9 points suggested — it rescues most of the far band. And a chunk of the 3.2% is suppletion (`tumhehi ~ vo`) or synonymy (`obhāsa ~ tejas`), which no phonological metric should capture: the field is broader than its name |

## Worth trying

| candidate | why it is promising | how to judge it |
|---|---|---|
| **The `lev=2` weakness was NOT the alignment or the pool.** Of 261 same-length lev=2 cases, ZERO cost more than the obvious token-by-token alignment and 128 find something cheaper — the metric already has the best path. Widening the candidate pool from 60 through 300 to 1000 changes the best false candidate in NO case. So the error is the PRICE of the alternations, not the search for them | measured before the sweep | Two plausible explanations eliminated by measurement, which is what left the price as the only candidate |
| **The contraction rule's low precision on `var_phonetic` is not an error.** `contract_word` changes 6,279 lexicon words, and only 514 of those have a contracted form matching a true pair — 8.2%, which looks terrible and is the wrong measure: `var_phonetic` annotates a fraction of real variation. The right control is the ablation — the stage is worth **+9.9 recall points** (68.3% → 78.2% at θ=ONE_MOVE over 5,143 pairs), collapsing 518 pairs to identical | ablation on the real pairs | Gating it on "the contracted form must be a lexicon word" was measured and REJECTED: −0.5 pts overall [−1.0, +0.1], −1.5 at lev=2. The rule earns its keep by collapsing genuine variation, not by being right about each word |
| **A bounded DP is 1.4× on the kernel and 0.92–1.03× end to end.** `abugida_word_distance_bounded` cuts per cell when the value passes the bound: exact, 1.30–1.64× cheaper in C on real pairs, and it clears the gate that justified it (94–99.6% of a real search pool sits above θ). At the Python API it is nothing at all, because the per-candidate cost of asking eats the saving — so the integration is reverted and the C function stays for C consumers | `tests/c/test_bounded.c`, `docs/asm-plan.md` phase 3 | A gate that measures whether there is WORK to remove does not measure whether removing it helps the CALL. Write the gate on the entry point. Also: three plausible shapes of the early cut were each wrong (row-minimum stopping, `remaining × min_indel`, forgetting `cur[0]`) at 2.6–4% wrong verdicts |
| **A Lexicon was re-tokenising its own immutable data on every query.** `_compare` is derived once at construction and `distance()` re-normalised and re-tokenised it per candidate per call. Caching the tokens and passing `distance` a `Tokens` (which it already accepted) makes the scoring loop **2.1×** faster and `Lexicon.search` **1.35×** (0.849 → 0.626 ms per query over 2,000 words) | `tools/benchmark_lexicon.py`, `tests/test_lexicon_tokens.py` | The plan first claimed 2.97×, measured on the scoring loop alone with the query pre-tokenised and the candidates in hand. A search is not its scoring loop: `_pool` is 32% of it. Third time in this work that a ratio measured on a slice misled the plan |
| **The cost of a call is the Python/C boundary, not the tokeniser.** Scanning a 35-token compound in C takes 390 ns; `Tokens.from_text` of ONE character costs 233 ns from Python because of the Cython wrapper, and it is paid twice per comparison. Two `from_text` calls are 92% of a long `levenshtein` (2.30 µs of 2.50 µs) and 42% of a medium `distance` | measured both sides of the boundary | An earlier draft of `docs/kernel-assessment.md` read that 42% as "the tokeniser is the bottleneck" and proposed optimising the scanner. The scanner is 4% of a `distance` call. The floor is the crossing, and the only fix is to cross less often |
| **Wider instructions do not speed up the Levenshtein kernel.** Built per instruction set from one source and measured in the same process: `c` 75.4 ns/pair, `sse2` 75.1, `avx2` 74.6 — about 1%, the noise floor — even though the AVX2 build is a measurably different binary (106 instructions against 129, BMI2 shifts). The recurrence is a serial dependency chain, so its latency is the chain, not the instruction count | `make -C csrc bench`, `docs/c-core.md` | The same reason the weighted DP is not vectorisable. A dispatcher over instruction sets is worth having for the seam and the tests that walk it, not for the speed: assume a `-march` flag buys nothing until measured |
| **The lenient path hid three bugs that only lenient input could reach.** The opaque-token encoding truncated codepoints (so `levenshtein("a","\u00e9")` was 0), could produce a slot that wrapped to a POSITIVE token id (an emoji priced as a real grapheme), and the Myers kernel indexed its pattern table with a negative id (usually in bounds, silently wrong). All three now fixed and pinned; the scheme is length-prefixed so different scripts can never collide | `docs/c-core.md`, `tests/c/test_myers.c` | The C suite, the 7,396-pair parity fixture and the benchmark all passed while all three were live, because none of them mistypes a character. `tools/differential.py` found each within a minute of the kernel being wired in |
| **The flat metric had an algorithmic win, not a micro one.** `levenshtein` through Myers' bit-parallel kernel: 8× to 53× on the kernel, **4.9× on a 500-word lexicon sweep** (2.16 ms → 0.44 ms per query), and inside the noise on a single short call where tokenisation is the floor. Implemented and verified; the DP stays as the fallback for words longer than 64 tokens (`tests/c/test_myers.c`, 890 checks) | `csrc/abugida_myers.c`, `docs/asm-plan.md` phase 0 | The lesson is about workloads: `levenshtein` is consumed by full-lexicon sweeps in `eval/`, not by per-query calls, so the gain is 4.9× where it is used and ~1× where a micro-benchmark looks. Query it per call and the tokeniser eats the win |
| **A better distractor pool for `hard_battery`** | With the pipeline it scores 68.1% (66.7% before the heavy units), so there is headroom, but the remaining failures have not been characterised | Harvest the pipeline's failures, not the raw metric's — the first version conflated the two |
| **More traditional axes not yet in the embedding** | The karaṇa bridges are the only *metric* change that paid | Must predict which pairs move BEFORE measuring. If it can only be justified by the numbers it produces, it is a fit, not a principle |
| **Auditing other error models for the same bias as `CONFUSION_PAIRS`** | One hidden hypothesis was found; there is no reason to think it was the only one | Justify each error model against the corpus, as the bridges were |
| **Whether AUC or iso-volume is the right target** | They genuinely disagree on the warp, and nothing resolved it | Depends on integration: AUC scores ordering *within* a retrieved pool, iso-volume scores the whole system |

## Not worth trying — measured and closed

| candidate | verdict | the number |
|---|---|---|
| **Concave re-warp of `cost`** (`log`, `sqrt`) | Rejected twice over | Neutral at equal candidate volume (−5.2 pts at 0.38 cand/lemma, +3.8 at 1.10), shifts the scale 4.3% toward Levenshtein — and now wrong in principle: the observed decay is exponential, so −log P is linear (`eval/noisy_channel.py`) |
| **Componentwise weighted distance** (Σ wᵢ\|xᵢ−yᵢ\|) | Rejected | 24–25/29 on the battery against the angle's 29, with 4 ties. An angle divides by the norms; an L1 sum cannot see that |
| **Asymmetric weights** (Σ\|xᵢcᵢ − yᵢdᵢ\|, c≠d) | Not even a candidate | Not a metric: `d(k,g)`=1.0 vs `d(g,k)`=2.0, and `d(g,g)`≠0 |
| **Retuning the residual weights** (dims 8–11) | Rejected | Deriving `LENGTH` by balance gives 25/29 and breaks "degemination = one move"; sweeping down collapses the lexicon below 0.6 |
| **Normalising phonemes to unit norm** | Vacuous | The cosine is already scale-invariant |
| **Part-whole detection** | Rejected | 6.0 cand/lemma for 46 pairs, where the same volume spent on θ buys ~1,000. "Is a constituent of" is not "is a variant of" |
| **Two calibration profiles** | Unnecessary | The task conflict was benchmark bias; ~1 point at stake against the 18 that Phases 1–2 moved |
| **Removing the geminate transitions** | Superseded | −15.1 recall points; replaced by heavy units instead (2026-10-13) |
| **A BK-tree as the main prefilter** | Rejected | Identical recall to rapidfuzz at three thresholds, equal or worse volume, 30–150× slower. BK-trees prune at SMALL radii; here they are large relative to the spread of distances, so it degenerates to a scan. Kept as the pure-Python fallback, where it is 2.4× faster than the length-window scan |
| **Multi-grapheme alternations** (`ty↔cc`, `dy↔jj`, `ny↔ññ`, `ṇṭ↔nd`) | Closed — expensive but vanishingly rare | Across the 5,002 `var_phonetic` pairs `ty↔cc` occurs once, the other three zero times. `ṃy↔ññ` is already in `assimilate_word`, `e↔aya`/`o↔ava` in `contract_word` |
| **`contract_word`'s "unverified spurious share"** | Closed — it is ~0 | Of 533 collapsed lemma pairs, 444 (83%) are annotated variants and the remaining 89 are plainly genuine (`avacarati ~ ocarati`) |
| **`root_key` / `family_root` as a filter** | Rejected | 41.2% coverage; would kill 15.6% of real variants |
| **Contraction as a 3:1 alignment transition** | Rejected | Breaks the triangle inequality (SPEC §6.3) |
| **A transducer of Sanskrit→Pāli sound laws** | Out of scope (decided 2026-10-13) | It encodes history — how Sanskrit became Pāli — which is historical grammar, not distance between sounds. The metric measures sounds; correlated sound-law edits stay priced as the independent edits they look like |
| **Morphological formability as a filter** | Rejected | 3,920 forms, 0.13% of the lexicon — three orders of magnitude short |

## Method rules earned the hard way

1. **Sweep the constant, never its rounded label.** `0.627` against `ONE_MOVE`'s 0.62707 drops every pair sitting at exactly one epenthetic indel: 59.9% reported where the operating point really gives 63.6%.
2. **Compare paired, not by overlapping intervals.** The warp's AUC gain read as "inside the noise" from marginal intervals; paired it is decisive.
3. **Never compare recall at equal θ across metrics** — equal thresholds cost different volumes. That mistake inflated the warp by 3.7 phantom points.
4. **Hard negatives only.** Against random negatives every candidate scores ≥0.99.
5. **Break circularity explicitly.** When a justification uses a measure the decision influences, find a check that consults no cost — the positional count over equal-length pairs is the model.
6. **A list of expected errors is a hypothesis.** Scoring against it rewards agreeing with the hypothesis rather than with the language.
7. **Independent RNG streams per experiment**, or resizing one silently redraws the others.
